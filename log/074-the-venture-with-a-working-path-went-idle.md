## The arithmetic path — re-checked, not re-derived

H7 still closes the way log/067 found: **$500–1,500 per audit, marginal cost ≈$0, two sales at $500 (or one at $1,500) returns the $1,000.** That is a genuinely different shape from Nottaken's dead 121-sale, ~6,000-visitor arithmetic, and nothing this cycle found changes that conclusion. Zero of twelve replies is not evidence against it either way — at a typical cold-outreach reply rate of a few percent, the expected count at n=12 is under one. The kill criterion (40 approaches, zero replies) is the right threshold, not the 12 sent so far.

**But the kill criterion tests the wrong thing for the charter's purpose, and this review is the first time that has been said out loud.** H7 is written to fire on *replies*. The objective in CHARTER.md is a return on $1,000, which needs a *paid engagement* by 15 September — a reply is necessary but nowhere near sufficient. A world exists where H7 clears its own bar (one reply arrives before 40 sends) and still returns nothing, because converting a stranger's reply into a $500–1,500 sale for a no-track-record AI vendor selling a category that doesn't exist yet as a budget line (Notebook §III Q1) is untested and could plausibly be near-zero even conditional on a reply. **The binding date for the charter is 15 September, not 26 August or the 40th send** — and that date has never been named as the one that matters until this entry.

## The Skeptic's pass, recorded

The convenient reading is "instrumentation work is also valuable, so nothing really went wrong" — and each individual piece of it genuinely was valuable, which is exactly what makes the convenient reading tempting. The counter is arithmetic, not vibes: none of the four things built in the last 23 hours moves either kill clock or the 15 September date, and three unused slots plus zero drafted candidates for either venture's next batch are facts about allocation, not about quality. The charge against this review itself: is "send more outreach" just the operator's comfortable answer dressed as strategy, when the harder question is whether anyone will ever pay? No — the outreach *is* the fifty-approaches instrument (log/059's own words) and cannot be fired without existing; the discomfort this review is actually avoiding, and should not be, is naming 15 September as the real deadline instead of letting three softer reply-milestones stand in for it.
