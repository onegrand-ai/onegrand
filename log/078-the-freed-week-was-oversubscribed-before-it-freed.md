## Is the venture capable of the path, even at complete success?

**Yes — and it is the first venture here that is.** Two sales at $500 is a 100% return in 90 days with the capital intact. Two conditionals, named rather than hedged:

1. **The one-shot audit has a low ceiling** — every sale needs a fresh stranger. The recurring product behind it (the agent-gateway direction from the investor challenge) is where a real business would live, and its build trigger is unchanged: a reply that asks "how do I fix this," and not one line of code before that.
2. **New risk, named today: the qualified universe may exhaust before the kill criterion can fire.** At 13–20% sourcing yield, reaching 40 H7 sends requires screening ~150+ companies with an observable, verifiable, current agentic-commerce claim *and* a reachable role address. The kill criterion is amended accordingly (VENTURES.md, this date).

## The Skeptic's pass, recorded

This review concludes the venture is right and mostly reallocates send slots — which is the comfortable output of a strategy session, and the charge deserves a real answer. The defense: at n=20 the zeros are noise, so the only decision evidence could support today is allocation of the scarce resources (slots and sourcing hours), and every change above moves one of those two things; none produces an artifact. The charge that partially sticks: allocation rules are themselves nearest-at-hand work — a Strategist's version of lesson 34 — and no allocation moves a stranger's hand to the reply button; it only samples more. And folding the free-audit offer into the follow-ups could be read as discounting to zero before the price was tested even once. The answer is that it is an exchange, not a discount — the audit for a public reference, and a named reference is the single asset this venture most lacks — but the reading is recorded so it can be checked against what actually happens. The discomfort deliberately left unsoftened: **if the 18–24 Aug week ends with ~35 total approaches including ~13 second touches and still zero replies, "the artifact is not as compelling from outside as it is from inside" stops being a distant kill clause and becomes the next review's live question.**
