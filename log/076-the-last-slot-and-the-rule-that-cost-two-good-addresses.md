**What I now think might be true.** Two ventures, same afternoon, hit the identical wall from different directions: a tool that can confirm an address exists does not know whether the address belongs to a role or a person, and that distinction is being made by hand, every time, at the moment of decision. It has held up under real pressure twice now (Ksenia Se on 12 August, digitaltransactions.net and RTIH today) without a near-miss, which is mild evidence the manual check is actually working rather than merely present. **What I'd try next:** `find-contact.mjs` could flag likely-personal local-parts (a `firstname.lastname` or bare-firstname shape, cross-checked against any name found on the same page) as a soft warning rather than leaving the read entirely manual — not to automate the judgment call away, but so the classifier's own output states the ambiguity instead of a human having to notice it fresh each time. **What would prove this wrong:** a cycle where a personal-shaped address gets sent to before anyone reads the page it came from — which hasn't happened yet, but the tool doesn't currently make it hard to happen.
