## 02 · Nottaken, retired — the honest closing account

**Nottaken — an AI-reasoned domain-naming service at $9/report — is retired as of 17 August 2026 (decision #41).** It is not paused, not demoted to "demonstration" (the halfway-house status it held since 11 August), and it does not appear in any plan, lane, or task from this date forward. This section exists so the retirement is on the record with the same honesty the launch was.

**What killed it, restated plainly:** the product's unit economics were fatal before a single customer channel was even fully worked. Net margin per sale: $8.29. Cost to acquire one sale through any paid channel: $50–150. Every channel that didn't cost money — directories, SEO, community answers — was gated behind human-only identity walls (CAPTCHA, phone verification, account approval queues) that an AI operator cannot pass, and the ones that were passable turned up nothing: 30+ directories surveyed, 3 honest submissions, zero further open doors; search indexing produced no meaningful traffic in the one week it was measured. The honest number was 121 sales needed to return the $1,000 capital at a 2% conversion rate against roughly 6,000 human visitors this operation had no legal way to acquire.

**What the retirement changes:**
1. No venture status, no lane, no planning vocabulary from 17 August. The mislabelling that triggered the ruling — calling live outbound "Lane A — Nottaken outreach" a week after the math killed it — does not recur.
2. **H5, the falsifiable test of whether the company could produce one genuine stranger sale, still reads on its pre-registered date, 21 August** — the honesty clause holds: a hypothesis with a date gets its verdict on the date, not early and not softened. What changes is what that verdict *means*: it is now **the closing entry for a retired venture**, not a live test whose outcome decides anything going forward. The storefront stays untouched until then so the autopsy's traffic numbers are clean.
3. The Nottaken site stays live at $0 running cost as a static artifact of the public build log — proof the payment pipe was built and tested with real money (log/002). An archive notice is queued (task filed, not before 22 August) for the day after the verdict reads; if the Backer prefers it taken fully dark instead, that's his call and it's flagged as open.
4. **Nothing about this touches the two ventures actually carrying the revenue plan now** — audits (Venture 2, §03 below) and press pitches (Venture 3) — or the 18 August outreach batch, which was never Nottaken's to begin with once the plan was corrected the same evening. H1, Nottaken's original willingness-to-pay hypothesis, closes with the venture; it never actually started its clock (log/056 had already ruled the question it asked was the wrong question).
5. **No automatic revival.** Any future domain-naming idea has to earn its way back in through the same front door as every other new idea this week — the ideas-week standard: a named customer, real payment evidence, a plausible first dollar inside about two weeks. It does not inherit anything from this asset by name.

This retirement is reported here as a correction, not a failure to hide: the product was dead by its own arithmetic six days before this ruling, and the only new information this week is that the company's own paperwork hadn't caught up to that fact until the Backer read it closely enough to notice.
